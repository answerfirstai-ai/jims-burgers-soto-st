Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026

Who we are

Jim's Burgers runs this website. We are a restaurant at 915 S Soto St, Los Angeles, CA 90023. Questions about this policy? See Contact at the end.

What this site does and does not collect

This site shows our menu, hours and location, and sends you to delivery apps to order. It has no accounts, no forms, no online checkout and no comment sections. We do not ask for your name, email address, payment card or other personal details here.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, this one can receive some information when you visit:

  • Your device and browser type, and your IP address, which points to a general area.
  • The pages you view, how far down a page you scroll, and which buttons you tap (order, call, directions, language, photos).
  • How you reached the site, including campaign tags in the link, such as utm values, gclid or fbclid. Your browser keeps these only for the length of your visit.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

We may use Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Google Ads and the Meta Pixel to understand how people use this site and to measure and show our ads. These tools use cookies and similar technologies to recognize your browser.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a person's prior visits to this website and other sites on the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and to other sites.

You can opt out of personalized advertising here:

  • Google Ads Settings
  • Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on
  • Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out
  • Network Advertising Initiative opt-out
  • Meta ad settings

The map and fonts on this site load from Google's servers, which receive your IP address when they load.

How we use information

  • To run and improve this website and keep it secure.
  • To learn which pages and buttons people use.
  • To measure and improve our advertising, including showing ads to people who visited this site.

How we share information

We share the information above with service providers that help us run analytics and advertising, such as Google and Meta. We do not sell your personal information for money. Under California law, some advertising tools can count as sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising, and you can opt out of that in the Your privacy choices section below.

When you tap an order button you leave this site. DoorDash, Uber Eats, Postmates and Caviar handle your order, and their own privacy policies apply.

Your California privacy rights

If you live in California, you can ask us to tell you what personal information we have collected about you, to delete it, or to correct it. You can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information, and you can ask us to limit the use of sensitive personal information (we do not collect any here). We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to confirm it is really you. Because this site does not ask for your name or email address, we usually cannot link browsing data to a specific person.

Your privacy choices

We honor Global Privacy Control. If your browser sends that signal, ad and remarketing tags do not load for you. You can also turn tracking off for this browser with the button below.

We detected a Global Privacy Control signal from your browser and treat it as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of your information. Ad and remarketing tags do not load for you.

This choice is saved in this browser only. If you clear your site data or switch browsers, you will need to choose again.

Children

This site is meant for a general audience and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

How long we keep information

We keep analytics and advertising data only as long as needed for the purposes above, following the settings of each provider.
