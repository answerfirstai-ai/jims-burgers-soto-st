Good to know.

What are Jim's Burgers' hours?

Jim's Burgers is open every day from 8 AM to 9 PM.

Where is Jim's Burgers located?

Jim's Burgers is at 915 S Soto St, Los Angeles, CA 90023.

Does Jim's Burgers deliver?

Yes. You can order delivery or pickup through DoorDash, Uber Eats, Postmates or Caviar, or through Jim's order page hosted by DoorDash. Fees and delivery times are set by each app.

Can I dine in or get takeout?

Yes. Jim's Burgers offers dine-in, takeout and delivery.

What is on the menu?

Quarter-pound charbroiled burgers including the hamburger, cheeseburger, bacon, double, chili, pastrami and colossal burgers, plus jr. burgers, a fried chicken sandwich, chicken nuggets and strips, a turkey burger, fish tacos, chili cheese fries, D.U.I. fries and onion rings.

How much does a meal cost?

Diners report $10-20 per person. Prices are posted on the menu board in the photos on this page and can change, so call (323) 269-9732 to confirm.

Does Jim's Burgers take credit cards?

Yes. A charge applies to credit card purchases, so ask when you order.

How do I order by phone?

Call (323) 269-9732 during opening hours, 8 AM to 9 PM every day.
